My CV (Updated: 2026-08-12)


Welcome! I am about to become a Ph.D. student in the Linguistics program at the University of Oregon in the fall of 2026. My advisor is Professor Kristopher Kyle at the Learner Corpus Research and Applied Data Science (LCR-ADS) lab.

My research encompass learner corpus research and natural language processing (NLP) for exploring L2 production data. Some recent directions are:

  • Linguistic annotation and training NLP pipelines for fine-grained senses disambiguation of constructions (and words) for LCR
  • Development of NLP pipelines and tools for LCR with languages other than English, more specifically, Korean and Chinese
  • Evaluation and improvement of NLP tools for downstream feature identification and indices calculation on written and spoken data
